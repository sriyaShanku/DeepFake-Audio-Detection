How Deepfake Audio Detection Works

Behind the scenes of our deepfake detection engine.

🔊

1. Upload Audio

Audio is loaded, normalized, and silent regions are trimmed for optimal processing.

🔬

2. Feature Extraction

MFCCs, spectral contrast, chroma, and ZCR are extracted to build a comprehensive audio profile.

🧠

3. ML Classification

A trained Random Forest classifier evaluates the features to determine real vs. fake.

📊

4. Results & Score

A confidence score and risk level are generated to help you make an informed decision.

Feature Extraction Deep Dive

MFCC (40 coefficients)

Mel Frequency Cepstral Coefficients capture the shape of the vocal tract. AI-generated voices often produce subtly different MFCC patterns than real human speech.

Spectral Contrast

Measures the difference in amplitude between peaks and valleys in the sound spectrum. Deepfake audio tends to have more uniform spectral patterns.

Chroma Features

Analyzes pitch class energy distribution across the 12 pitch classes. Reveals harmonic characteristics unique to natural speech.

Zero Crossing Rate

Measures how often the signal crosses zero. Helps detect the rhythm, noise levels, and micro-variations that distinguish real from synthetic speech.
